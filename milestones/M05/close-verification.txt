WHY THIS WAS RE-RUN RATHER THAN CLOSED ON THE AUGUST CARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

M05's window ran live on 2026-08-20 at sha 1f46b92 and its LIFECYCLE half
passed end to end — `make up` gated at 1157/1157 twice, `make down` verified,
a new collection id on cycle 2, the fault-drop rollback self-repaired, and the
janitor tore the stack down through the restricted role. That evidence was
good and is not being replaced.

What was open was the `make evals` green criterion. Two things made closing on
the old cards wrong:

1. THE CARDS PREDATE M06 AND M07. They were recorded at 1f46b92, before the
   load work, before the governance layer, before the eval gate's bar changed.
   Closing a milestone on evidence recorded against a tree that has since moved
   is the exact defect found in `milestones/M07/ci_eval_role_mutations.py` on
   the same day this window was re-run — an artifact still asserting a result
   about code that changed underneath it. Doing it deliberately, hours later,
   would have been worse than doing it by accident.

2. THE OLD CARDS ARE STILL RED UNDER THE NEW BAR. M07's PM ruling
   (`milestones/M07/eval-gate-bar-ruling.md`) replaced `passed == total` with a
   regression bar. Under it the 2026-08-20 S3 Vectors run is STILL red: q03
   failed there and q03 has passed before, so it scores as a REGRESSION, not as
   a known-bad. Making that green on paper would require the admitted-false-fail
   register to reach `run_evals.gate_verdict()` — which it does not, and which
   M07 deliberately left open (`milestones/M07/eval-gate-flake-gap.md`).
   Closing M05 on that reading would have quietly decided an open question in
   the direction that suited the close.

So: re-run, at today's code, under the bar the PM seat actually ruled.
