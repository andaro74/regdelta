5. WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

ESTABLISHED. q05's decline is a PARSE FAILURE IN `verdict()`. It is not a
throttle, not a timeout, not load, not concurrency, not position in the run,
not the corpus, not the question, and not the ground truth. `_json_object`
could not parse a complete 764-token model reply, returned `{}`, and every
downstream field collapsed to its empty value. The HITL gate then did its job
correctly on an empty answer, and `run_evals.py` then reported a regression
correctly. BOTH GATES BEHAVED AS DESIGNED. The system under them did not.

STILL A HYPOTHESIS, and labelled as one. WHY that reply did not parse is not
established, because the raw text is logged nowhere and cannot be recovered
after the fact. The leading candidate is that `json.loads` is strict about
literal control characters inside strings, and 764 tokens is the LONGEST q05
completion observed — longer than all six that parsed. A longer prose answer
is a likelier place for a raw newline to land inside the "answer" string. That
is a guess with a shape, not a measurement, and the next person should treat
it exactly the way this document's first three framings deserved to be
treated.

  AND IT IS WRONG. Falsified the same day, one run later — see section 7.
  Left standing above rather than deleted, because the guess and its
  refutation are two hours apart and the pattern is the whole point of this
  file.

6. THE INSTRUMENT THAT WOULD HAVE SAID SO ALREADY EXISTS AND IS NOT READ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

nodes.py:126-131 predicted this failure class in writing:

  "`_json_object` returns {} when it cannot find a closing brace, so a
   max_tokens cut-off mid-object produces an EMPTY ANSWER with status "ok"
   — the pause-shaped failure, indistinguishable from a model that had
   nothing to say. The stop reason is the field that tells those apart."

M05 and ADR-0013 built that field. `verdict()` returns `stop_reason` and
`truncated`; `instrument.py` carries both; `api.py` puts them in the response
body. And then:

  - `run_evals.py` never prints either one on a failure, so the scorecard
    that blocked two pull requests could not say the model had stopped
    normally.
  - `q05_probe.py` never printed either one, so the probe could not say it
    either — and the probe was written specifically to separate hypotheses.
  - Nothing writes the raw completion anywhere, so the one artifact that
    would settle section 5 is gone.

The instrument was built for precisely this and is pointed at nothing. That is
why three successive framings of the gap document named a symptom: the reading
that separates them was sitting in the response body the whole time and no
consumer looked at it.

It also means the prediction in that comment was HALF right. It named the
max_tokens cut-off. This is its sibling: a COMPLETE reply that does not parse.
`stop_reason` distinguishes the two. `truncated` alone would have read False
here and sent the reader back to the beginning.
